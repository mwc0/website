Nothing to sign up for.

The whole site is a handful of static files. Here is what it does, and what it keeps.

Accounts
None. There is no login anywhere on the site.
Where it runs
In your browser. The games and tools are plain scripts, with nothing to install.
Windows
Everything opens in a window you can drag, resize and stack, so a converter can sit beside a game.
High scores
Snake keeps one shared board. A run that makes the top ten earns three initials, arcade style.
Currency
Live exchange rates, with a built-in table to fall back on if the request fails.
Kept on your device
Your best scores, your initials and your theme choice. They stay in this browser.
Counted
Google Analytics counts page views, and a live counter shows how many people are here right now.
